If you found your way here, thanks for showing up early.

This is **Field Reports on AI Agents** — a monthly newsletter where I track down working professionals (finance, data, ops, supply chain, planning) and ask them the same set of questions:

- What did you actually use AI for this month?
- What's the prompt you'd never delete?
- What broke?

Then I write it up, publish the prompts in full, and ship it to your inbox.

## Why I'm doing this

I'm an industrial engineer at Texas Instruments, moving into business planning in April 2026. Every day I push AI agents at real supply-chain work — sometimes they hold, sometimes they don't. The wins are useful. The failures are *more* useful, but nobody writes those up.

So I started asking other working professionals what they were doing. Turns out a lot of people are quietly building serious workflows — they just don't have time to publish them.

That's the gap. I'll publish them.

## What's coming in May

- An interview with a senior FP&A analyst who replaced two days of monthly variance analysis with a Claude Code workflow
- A field note from my own desk: how I'm using sub-agents to draft quarterly business reviews
- A transcript-style writeup with a data engineer who automated 80% of his on-call triage

## How to get the most out of this

Read the prompts. Copy them. Modify them. Reply with what worked or what didn't.

If you forward an issue to a colleague, that's the highest compliment.
