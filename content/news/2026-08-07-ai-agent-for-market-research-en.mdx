AI agents help with market research across two separable jobs: gathering and synthesizing the evidence — desk research, industry reports, survey data — and deciding what it means for your strategy. AlphaSense, Perplexity, and Qualtrics now automate the first job well. The second — the strategic call — stays human.

## The market-research workflow, and which parts an agent can take

Market research is a pipeline: gather the evidence (desk research, industry reports, survey data), synthesize it into a picture of the market, and decide what that picture means for what you build, price, or pitch. An agent is genuinely useful for the first two stages — the gathering is slow and repetitive, and the synthesis is a draft a human reviews. The part to keep human is the *decision*. So the honest test for any "AI agent for market research" pitch is to ask, at each stage, *does the agent do the work, or make the call?* It's the same automate-the-work-not-the-decision line we drew for [consultants](/en/news/2026-07-29-ai-agents-for-consultants/) and [financial analysts](/en/news/2026-07-31-ai-agents-for-financial-analysts/) — and [competitive analysis](/en/news/2026-08-06-ai-agent-for-competitive-analysis/) is one slice of this same job.

## Gather — pull the desk research and the survey data

The front of the pipeline is collection, and it splits into two kinds. Secondary research — reading what already exists — is where a research agent clearly beats tab-by-tab searching. [AlphaSense's Deep Research](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alphasense-launches-deep-research-automating-in-depth-analysis-with-agentic-ai-on-high-value-content-302476710.html) runs dozens of searches across millions of source documents — filings, broker research, expert-call transcripts — and returns a structured company primer or market landscape instead of a link list. For live web trends, [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) answers a research question by searching the current web and returning a summary with a citation on each claim, so you can check the source. The other kind is primary research — your own survey and interview data — where AI is now embedded directly in the research platform: [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/market-research/) builds survey design, analysis, and conversational follow-up into the tool itself.

## Synthesize — turn sources into a landscape, keep the framing yours

A pile of documents and survey exports isn't a finding; someone has to shape it into the market picture a decision rests on. This is the second job agents now take. AlphaSense's January 2026 Generative Search added [Workflow Agents](https://help.alpha-sense.com/hc/en-us/articles/48824062007187-AlphaSense-Product-Updates-January-2026) that automate entire arcs of analysis — building a company primer, a competitive landscape, or a SWOT — compressing what used to be days of manual assembly into minutes. Treat the output the way you'd treat any agent draft: a strong first pass an analyst reviews, not a finished claim. It's the same research-to-asset discipline behind [competitive analysis](/en/news/2026-08-06-ai-agent-for-competitive-analysis/) — automate the draft, own what ships.

## Decide — the strategic call stays human

A market landscape is only as useful as the judgment that reads it, and that judgment is exactly where an agent is weakest. The [Harvard–BCG field experiment we covered for consultants](/en/news/2026-07-29-ai-agents-for-consultants/) found that on tasks needing integrative business judgment, professionals using GPT-4 were *more* likely to reach the wrong conclusion — confident, well-formatted, and wrong. Deciding which market to enter, how to position, or what the data says you should build is that kind of task. Even the industry's own optimists frame it this way: in [Qualtrics' 2026 Market Research Trends report](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/research-teams-not-using-ai-are-four-times-more-likely-lose-organizational-influence/) — a survey of more than 3,000 market-research professionals across 14 countries — 78% expect AI agents to run more than half of all research projects by 2028, yet the framing throughout is agents doing the legwork while researchers do the thinking. The agent compiles the evidence; what it means for your bet is the call you keep.

## Which agent should you start with?

Start where your evidence is thinnest. If you're flying blind on the outside market — competitors, industry shifts, analyst takes — begin with a desk-research agent like [AlphaSense](https://www.alpha-sense.com/solutions/generative-ai-in-market-research/) or [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) so a sourced brief lands instead of a blank page. If your gap is customer signal, start with a primary-research platform like [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/market-research/). ## FAQ

**What AI agents help with market research?**
Match the agent to the stage. For secondary and desk research, [AlphaSense Deep Research](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alphasense-launches-deep-research-automating-in-depth-analysis-with-agentic-ai-on-high-value-content-302476710.html) pulls filings, broker research, and expert calls into a structured brief, and [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) summarizes live web trends with citations. For primary research, [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/market-research/) builds AI into survey design and analysis. The strategic read of what the findings mean stays with you.

**Can an AI agent do my market research for me?**
It can do the gathering and the first-draft synthesis — dozens of searches, a company primer, a competitive landscape, a survey summary. What it can't do reliably is the judgment call the research exists to inform. [AlphaSense's Workflow Agents](https://help.alpha-sense.com/hc/en-us/articles/48824062007187-AlphaSense-Product-Updates-January-2026) build the landscape; deciding what to do about it is the part you own.

**What's the difference between primary and secondary research for an agent?**
Secondary research is reading what already exists — reports, filings, the web — and it's where agents like AlphaSense and Perplexity shine. Primary research is data you collect yourself, through surveys and interviews, where a platform like [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/market-research/) embeds AI in the collection and analysis. Most real projects use both; you can automate the desk work without automating the survey design.

**Do I need an enterprise platform, or can I do this at desk scale?**
Both work. AlphaSense and Qualtrics are enterprise suites, but the underlying pattern — pull a bounded set of sources, summarize, draft a checkable asset — is reproducible with the same setup behind [Issue #001](/en/posts/2026-05-13-gmail-ai-agent/). Start small if you're a small team; the [no-code AI agent tools](/en/news/2026-07-08-no-code-ai-agent-tools/) guide shows what to wire a research step into.

**Where should a small team start?**
With the stage that costs you the most. If you keep guessing about the market, stand up a desk-research agent and read a sourced brief each week. If you're guessing about customers, start with a survey platform. Layer, don't boil the ocean — and keep the strategic call human at every step.
